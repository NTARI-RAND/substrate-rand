# Tier 1: The SohoCloud Protocol

*The goal of Tier 1 is to understand the relationship of prosumer hardware to the carriers whose networks it runs on.*

Nodes join by reaching out, without open inbound ports or a fixed address. Work that settles a spend or enters the record runs on at least two independent machines. See P1-004, Routed, Not Negotiated.
