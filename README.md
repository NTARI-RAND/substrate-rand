# substrate-rand

Research & Development on the Substrate Layer of the Janus Facing Architecture.

The hardware where everything happens: computers, GPUs, printers, storage and sensors owned by prosumers. Leaving is cheap, so competition does the disciplining.

Research is discussed in #rand-substrate and voted on in #federation-substrate in the NTARI Slack workspace.

## Structure

| Tier | Goal |
|---|---|
| [Tier 1: The SohoCloud Protocol](tier-1-sohocloud-protocol/) | Understand the relationship of prosumer hardware to the carriers whose networks it runs on |
| [Tier 2: The SoHoLINK Orchestrator](tier-2-soholink-orchestrator/) | Understand how coordination across geography works as an open market |
| [Tier 3: The Cloudy Platform](tier-3-cloudy-platform/) | Implement the protocol and the JFA standard in the interface where prosumers offer and buy compute and storage |

## How we research

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your file to the correct tier folder.
3. Create a pull request with a [DCO signature](https://github.com/apps/dco).
4. Wait for peer review.

#federation-substrate has 90 days to deliberate your publication, during which you defend your claims and make the changes reviewers ask for. Follow the [Research & Development Submission Guide](https://ntari.slack.com/docs/T0978JCFFSL/F0BTB5N93A9): the method, the ethics check and the submission template.

Merged research is published in #federation-volunteers, on [www.ntari.org](https://www.ntari.org) and affiliated social media channels, with the author(s) credited.
