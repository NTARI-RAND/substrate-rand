# Tier 2: The SoHoLINK Orchestrator

*The goal of Tier 2 is to understand how coordination across geography works as an open market.*

Orchestrators publish transport offers naming a fee, a delivery promise and a public key. Any platform may choose any orchestrator it can reach.
